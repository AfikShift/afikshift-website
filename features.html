Features

Everything you need to stay on top of your shifts and your pay.

Shift manager

Add, edit, duplicate, cancel and delete shifts. Month, week and list views with day and night markers.

Shift templates

Save your regular patterns – times, breaks, rate and shift type – and reuse them with one tap.

Overtime & custom types

Standard, overtime and your own shift types, each with its own hourly rate and unpaid break.

Earnings dashboard

Weekly, monthly and yearly views: worked so far, booked still to work and expected pay before tax, with an optional tax percentage estimate.

Payment tracking

Log full or partial payments against each shift and see what is still outstanding or overdue.

Works offline

Record shifts without signal. Changes sync automatically when you are back online, with clash handling across devices.

Private & secure

Records are encrypted on your phone, sent over HTTPS, and protected by optional fingerprint or face unlock. No adverts, no tracking.

Your data, your copy

Download a JSON copy of your records once every seven days on every plan, or request another copy by email. Export CSV and PDF reports with Pro.

Recently deleted

Deleted a shift by mistake? Restore it from Recently Deleted for up to 90 days.

AfikShift is a personal record-keeping tool. Earnings figures are estimates – always check them against your official payslip.
