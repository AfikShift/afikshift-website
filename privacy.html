4. How your data is protected

  • On your phone, shift and payment records are encrypted, with the key held in the phone's secure keystore.
  • Data travels between the app and our servers over HTTPS (TLS). Emergent states that its platform provides AES-256 encryption at rest and automatic encryption-key rotation.
  • Before public launch, AfikShift is being tested in a preview environment with a local test MongoDB database. Emergent has confirmed that on first production deployment, preview data is migrated to a managed MongoDB Atlas database, after which preview and production databases are separate. Emergent reports that the production database has automatic hourly and daily backups retained for seven days, weekly backups retained for four weeks, monthly backups retained for twelve months, yearly backups retained for one year, and a continuous seven-day point-in-time recovery window. These production arrangements are not active for the preview database. AfikShift’s separate application-level encrypted backup script is not scheduled. We have not independently tested restoration.
  • Fingerprint and face unlock are handled entirely by your phone. We never receive biometric data.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, shift and payment data: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Deleted shifts: kept in Recently Deleted for 90 days, then permanently removed.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days; access tokens expire after 15 minutes.
  • One-time codes and failed sign-in counters: approximately 15 minutes.
  • Deleted-account audit record (internal identifier, hashed email and date): 65 days.
  • Administrative action log: 365 days; store payment notifications: 90 days.
  • IP addresses are used for security and rate limiting; short-lived failed sign-in counters expire after 15 minutes. Infrastructure request logs may retain IP addresses for a different period.
  • Emergent application logs: 7 days under its standard documentation, or 30 days for Pro and Enterprise plans; the applicable AfikShift plan and retention should be verified. Server access and other infrastructure log retention: not yet confirmed by the provider.
  • Subscription records in the application are deleted when an account is deleted. Any separate statutory accounting records and their retention are subject to applicable statutory accounting requirements.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can request access to or a copy of your personal data by contacting us. The app provides a self-service JSON export once every seven days on all plans; for an additional export within that period, contact us by email. Pro also offers CSV and PDF reports. You can delete your account in the app or request assistance by emailing us. We normally respond to rights requests within one month, subject to applicable law.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), but please contact us first so we can try to help.
