2. Information we collect

We may collect and process:

  • Account information: name, email address, password hash, professional category, currency and account preferences.
  • Shift information: dates, start and finish times, shift types, locations, rates, unpaid breaks, overlapping hours, calculation adjustments, rounding preferences and shift templates.
  • Earnings information: estimated pay, tax percentages, payments received, outstanding balances and optional payment notes.
  • Subscription information: subscription plan, purchase status, renewal and cancellation information, payment-provider identifiers and relevant transaction records.
  • Referral information: referral codes, rewards and related activity.
  • Security information: session identifiers, verification records, failed sign-in attempts, IP addresses and technical request logs.

We do not require users to upload official payslips or confidential employer records.

3. Why we process information

We process account, shift, earnings and subscription information to provide the services users request, generally under the lawful basis of performing our contract with them.

We process security information, fraud-prevention records and relevant referral information where necessary for our legitimate interests in protecting AfikShift and its users.

We retain certain financial and transaction information where necessary to comply with legal obligations.

4. How AfikShift uses your records

AfikShift helps users organise shifts, calculate estimated payable hours, record unpaid periods and track expected and received payments.

Where available, users may apply employer-specific calculation preferences. These settings are user-controlled and do not constitute official payroll rules.

AfikShift does not independently verify an employer's payroll calculations.

5. Storage and security

We use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, including encrypted connections, access controls and supported device security features.

Where supported, locally stored shift and payment information is encrypted using device-protected storage.

Fingerprint and facial authentication are processed by the user's device. AfikShift does not receive biometric templates.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

We will update this notice if our use of cookies or tracking technologies changes.

8. Data retention

Account, shift and payment information is generally retained while an account remains active, unless the user deletes relevant records or applicable law requires longer retention.

Deleted shifts may remain in the Recently Deleted feature for up to 90 days before permanent removal from the live service.

When an account is deleted, its active account records are removed. Before removal, we create a minimal deletion record containing only an internal account identifier, the date and the reason for deletion (no name, email address or shift and payment records). If this record cannot be created, the deletion is held as a pending request and completed once it can be.

Deletion records are kept for up to 15 months: the longest backup period (12 months) plus a three-month safety margin. They are used only to make sure deleted accounts are not reinstated if a backup is restored, and are deleted automatically afterwards.

Our hosting provider keeps encrypted backups of the database: point-in-time recovery for 7 days and snapshot backups for up to 12 months. Backup data is used only for recovery and is not routinely accessed as active account data.

If a backup is ever restored, the service is kept closed to users until our deletion records have been re-applied, so that accounts deleted after that backup was made are removed again before the service reopens.

Certain purchase and accounting records may be retained for up to six years, or longer where legally required.

Security logs are retained only for periods reasonably necessary for security, operational and legal purposes.

  • Request deletion or restriction.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Receive portable copies of eligible information.

You can access supported export and deletion features within AfikShift or contact contactus@afikshift.com.

We normally respond to valid data-protection requests within one month, subject to applicable extensions.

You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk.
