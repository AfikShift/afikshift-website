This policy explains how AfikShift Pro subscriptions are cancelled and how refund requests are handled.

2. Subscription plans

AfikShift Pro is offered through the following recurring plans:

  • Monthly: £3.99, renewing every month.
  • 6-Month: £19.99, renewing every six months.

The applicable price, billing frequency and renewal terms are displayed before purchase.

AfikShift subscription prices are displayed and charged in pounds sterling (GBP). Currency conversion charges may apply depending on your payment provider. Applicable taxes, where required, will be disclosed before payment.

3. Cancelling your subscription

You may cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future automatic renewals.

Unless applicable law requires otherwise, paid Pro access remains available until the end of the current billing period. Your account then returns to the Free plan.

Your shift and payment records are not automatically deleted when a Pro subscription ends.

Stripe — website or direct APK

Subscriptions are paid by card through Stripe. To cancel, open the AfikShift app and choose Account → Manage subscription. This opens Stripe's secure subscription-management page, where you can cancel future renewals.

Alternatively, email contactus@afikshift.com requesting cancellation. We will assist with cancellation through the appropriate payment system.

App stores

AfikShift subscriptions are not currently sold through Google Play, Aptoide or any other app store. If this changes, this policy will be updated with the relevant cancellation steps.

Important

Uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel a subscription.

Deleting an AfikShift account may not cancel a subscription managed by an external app store. Please cancel the subscription separately or contact us for assistance.

4. Your statutory cancellation rights

UK consumers may have a statutory right to cancel a distance contract within 14 days of entering into it, subject to the applicable rules and exceptions.

Where AfikShift Pro is legally classified as a service, and you expressly request performance during the cancellation period, applicable law may permit a proportionate charge for services supplied before cancellation, provided the required conditions are met.

Different rules may apply to digital content. We will not treat use of the app alone as sufficient to establish that you have waived statutory cancellation rights.

Any applicable statutory right to cancel remains available regardless of our ordinary subscription cancellation arrangements.

5. Refunds

We provide refunds where required by applicable consumer law.

We do not offer an additional voluntary money-back guarantee.

Outside statutory entitlements, payments for billing periods already started are generally non-refundable. However, we may consider discretionary refunds in exceptional circumstances, including duplicate or erroneous charges.

Approved refunds for card payments are issued through Stripe to the original payment method.

Nothing in this policy limits rights relating to faulty, misdescribed or improperly supplied digital services or content.

6. How to request cancellation or a refund

Email contactus@afikshift.com with:

  • Your AfikShift account email address.
  • The date of purchase.
  • The payment provider used.
  • The order or transaction reference, if available.
  • A brief explanation of your request.

Please do not send payment-card numbers or sensitive banking information.

We aim to acknowledge requests within two working days.

Where a refund is legally due, it will be processed without undue delay and within the applicable statutory deadline, ordinarily using the original payment method unless otherwise agreed.

7. Direct APK purchases

An AfikShift APK downloaded directly from our website is installed outside an app store.

Where a subscription is purchased through Stripe, its billing, cancellation and applicable refund arrangements are handled through Stripe and AfikShift rather than through Google Play or Aptoide.

Direct APK users may need to install updates through our official distribution channel.

8. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy excludes or restricts statutory consumer rights.

Consumers outside the United Kingdom may have additional mandatory rights under the laws of their country of residence.
