4. Your statutory cancellation rights

If you are a consumer in the UK, the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 usually give you the right to cancel an online contract within 14 days. For a subscription, the 14 days normally run from the day the contract is made.

Digital services

Where AfikShift Pro is supplied as a digital service, you may still cancel within the 14-day period even if you asked for the service to start straight away. In that case, we may deduct a proportionate amount for the service supplied up to the time you told us you were cancelling, and refund the rest.

Digital content

Where something is supplied as digital content (not on a physical medium), the law allows the 14-day cancellation right to end once supply has begun, but only if you gave your express consent to supply starting during the cancellation period, you acknowledged that you would lose your right to cancel, and we (or the store) confirmed this to you. If these conditions are not met, your right to cancel continues for the full 14 days.

Simply opening or using the app does not, on its own, mean you lose your cancellation rights.

Faulty or misdescribed services and content

Separately from the 14-day right, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives you rights if a service is not performed with reasonable care and skill, or if digital content is faulty, not as described or not fit for purpose. Depending on the circumstances, these may include a repair, a replacement, a price reduction or a refund.

To exercise a cancellation right, contact the store you purchased through, or email us with a clear statement that you wish to cancel (see section 6).

5. Refunds

  • Apart from that, payments for billing periods that have already started are not normally refunded, including for part-used periods after cancellation.
  • In exceptional circumstances, we may approve a discretionary refund, decided case by case. For example, if you were charged in error or charged twice.

Free Pro time from referrals or promotions has no cash value and cannot be refunded or exchanged.
